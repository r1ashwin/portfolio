Ashwin Deshpande · Qekto

Hey, I’m Ashwin. I’m 23 and I’m building Qekto.

I’m a software engineer at Amazon. Right after my internship, they picked me as one of two founding engineers for a new Prime Video Ads team, which handles ads data worldwide. Back in college, I published an ML research paper and ran a small hardware startup where I sold electromechanical clocks, drones, custom PCBs and other small electronics people asked me to build. I also headed my university’s robotics hub for three years, and raised over three lakh rupees by running workshops to keep it going.

Lately my workload on the new team has been really crazy, so I built a few AI agents, and just out of curiosity, one day I ran them on local models instead of frontier APIs. They were really good, even on big multimodal workflows. That’s when I realized companies can use open models for a lot of their work, stop depending on frontier labs, and own their models and tools entirely.

But what’s hard is everything around the model: understanding the workflow, collecting the right data, training with distillation, fine-tuning or reinforcement learning, trusting the model enough to ship it, and keeping it running as models, products and situations change. Today that takes a whole ML team.

Qekto does all of that for them, without one. Companies own their models, run them on their own servers, and Qekto keeps improving them so they always meet production needs.

I built a working version in two days. This is Qekto v1. Let me show you.
